# Product

## Register

product

## Users

An SAP account executive managing a mixed territory of installed-base customers, net-new prospects, partner-sourced accounts, and active opportunities. The app is used locally during daily account planning, follow-up, and spreadsheet consolidation.

## Product Purpose

Provide one dependable local workspace for finding accounts, maintaining contacts and next actions, importing inconsistent territory workbooks without duplicates, and understanding portfolio coverage at a glance.

## Brand Personality

Precise, calm, assured. The interface should feel professional and focused, with enough density for serious territory work and a restrained SAP-adjacent blue identity.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic SaaS landing-page styling, decorative dashboards, oversized metric cards, novelty interactions, glass effects, and enterprise software that buries common actions in modal-heavy workflows.

## Design Principles

- Put the next account action within one click.
- Preserve context when data is imported or edited.
- Make dense territory data easy to scan, filter, and compare.
- Explain system decisions, especially import detection and merging.
- Prefer familiar controls and reversible actions.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA with keyboard access, visible focus states, semantic labels, reduced-motion support, high text contrast, and status indicators that never rely on color alone.
